## 📌 Research Abstract & Overview
This academic research explores human-centric cybersecurity vulnerabilities and social engineering attack vectors (Phishing, Baiting, Pretexting) within commercial banking environments, focusing on Bank of Ceylon (BOC) operational scenarios. It analyzes human risk factors, security culture deficits, and organizational protocols, proposing a structured defense framework to bolster organizational cyber resilience.
